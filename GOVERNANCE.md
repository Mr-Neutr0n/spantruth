# Governance

wiretruth is maintained by [Plexito](https://plexito.de). The people with write access are listed
in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Decisions

- Maintainers merge pull requests after review. A change to a check, a verdict or a rule needs
  one maintainer approval and a test that fails without the change.
- A disputed finding is decided in public, in the issue that disputes it. The deciding question
  is always the same: does the cited rule say what the check claims? When the spec is unclear,
  the cell is marked as disputed and we ask the spec owners.
- The project publishes no ranking and no total score. It shows each finding with its evidence.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have made several substantial contributions and reviews can be invited as
maintainers. Maintainers of instrumentation libraries are especially welcome. Ask in an issue or
in Discussions.

## Neutrality

Plexito does not sell an instrumentation library or an observability backend. If that changes,
this section says so.
